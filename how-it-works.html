How it works

Capture. Remember. Remind. Complete.

One simple loop that helps you remember and finish everyday commitments without adding more mental work.

  1. CaptureWrite it naturally: “Pay rent on December 1st.”
  2. Understand and confirmMERVAIQ shows the title, due date, reminder time and notes. You check them and make changes.
  3. RememberYour commitment is saved and becomes searchable.
  4. RemindAt the time you chose, MERVAIQ sends your reminder.
  5. Follow throughComplete it, reschedule it, or keep it visible as overdue until it’s handled.

A promise we hold ourselves to: if you write “next Friday” and the date is unclear, MERVAIQ shows you the date it understood and asks you to confirm.

Capture it once. Find it when you need it. Follow through.
