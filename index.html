What MERVAIQ does

One place to remember, and follow through.

Capture it once

Type it the way you’d say it, like “Remind me to call Mum on Sunday at 6 PM.” You confirm the details before it’s saved.

Find it when you need it

Search your commitments and keep notes and context attached to each one.

Timely reminders

You choose when to be reminded, and MERVAIQ keeps your timezone in mind.

Follow through

Edit, reschedule or complete. Nothing is marked done unless you say so.

Built on trust

You stay in charge.

MERVAIQ is for remembering, not for taking over. If a date is unclear, it shows you what it understood and asks you to confirm instead of guessing. Your reminders are yours to control, and what you save is yours to edit or delete.

FAQ

Good questions

Who is MERVAIQ for?

We’re starting with young professionals living independently, who are juggling work, bills, appointments and errands.

Does MERVAIQ pay my bills?

No. It helps you remember them and follow through. It doesn’t make payments for you.

What if it misunderstands a date?

It shows you the date it understood and asks you to confirm before saving. You can always edit it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. MERVAIQ is a web app designed to work comfortably on mobile browsers as well as desktop.
